# Business-Intelligence-Dashboard

## ShopSphere Retail – Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard

Industry: E-commerce
Company Type: B2C Online Retail Marketplace
## Business Model:
ShopSphere is a fast-growing online retailer operating across multiple regions in India, offering products in categories such as Electronics, Fashion, Home & Living, Beauty, and Sports. The leadership team wants to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, promotional effectiveness, and regional trends to support strategic decision-making.

## Business Problem Statement

* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* Which regions are driving or losing sales?
* Who are our most valuable customers?
* Which payment methods are preferred?
* How can we improve customer retention?
* What are the expected sales for the next quarter?
