# ShopSphere Retail – Executive E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard

Developed an end-to-end Power BI dashboard for a fictional e-commerce company, **ShopSphere Retail**, to provide actionable business insights across sales, customers, products, and regional performance. The project followed a complete Business Intelligence lifecycle, starting with data ingestion from a CSV dataset containing 10,000 transaction records.

Performed data cleaning and transformation using **Power Query**, including data type validation, calculated fields, and data quality checks. Designed a **Star Schema** by creating a centralized fact table and multiple dimension tables (Date, Customer, Product, Region, and Payment Method) to ensure efficient data modeling and optimized report performance.

Built a dedicated **Measure Table** containing advanced DAX measures for key business metrics such as Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), Customer Count, Revenue Growth, YTD/MTD Sales, Running Total, Category Contribution, Repeat Customers, and RFM scores. Implemented **Time Intelligence** functions to analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.

Designed four interactive dashboard pages:

* **Executive Overview:** Revenue KPIs, sales trends, regional performance, and payment method analysis.
* **Product Analysis:** Category performance, contribution analysis, product rankings, and discount effectiveness.
* **Customer RFM Analysis:** Customer segmentation into Champions, Loyal, At Risk, and Lost Customers using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary analysis.
* **Revenue Analysis:** Revenue trends, regional comparisons, growth analysis, and business performance monitoring.

Enhanced the report with interactive features such as synchronized slicers, drill-through pages, bookmarks, custom tooltips, dynamic titles, conditional formatting, and field parameters to improve user experience and data exploration.

The dashboard enables business stakeholders to monitor overall performance, identify high-value customer segments, optimize product strategies, evaluate promotional effectiveness, and make data-driven decisions to improve revenue growth and customer retention.

## Business Questions 
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* Which regions are driving or losing sales?
* Who are our most valuable customers?
* Which payment methods are preferred?
* How can we improve customer retention?
* What are the expected sales for the next quarter?
